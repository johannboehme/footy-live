# Footy Live — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

Footy Live is an unofficial fan app for Even Realities G2 glasses that shows AFL live scores, fixtures and the ladder. It is built and maintained by Johann Boehme (contact: lorddevien@gmail.com).

## What the app collects

**Nothing personal.** Footy Live has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It does not collect, store or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, microphone audio, photos or device identifiers.

## Data stored on your device

The only thing the app saves is your **favourite club** (a short team code such as `COLL`), so the *My Team* page can show it. This is stored locally through the Even app's storage for this plugin and never leaves your phone. You can clear it by uninstalling the app.

## Network access

Footy Live requests the `network` permission for one purpose: fetching public AFL scores, fixtures and ladder data. Requests go only to:

- `https://site.api.espn.com` — ESPN's public sports data feeds

These requests contain no personal data beyond what any web request carries (your IP address, which ESPN receives as the server operator). ESPN's own privacy policy applies to their service. Footy Live has no backend server of its own; nothing is relayed through servers operated by the developer.

## Permissions summary

| Permission | Why it is requested | Data involved |
|-----------|--------------------|---------------|
| `network` (whitelist: `site.api.espn.com`) | Download live scores, fixtures and ladder | Public sports data only; no personal data sent |

No other permissions (location, microphone, camera, album) are requested or used.

## Children

The app contains no user-generated content and collects no data, and is suitable for all ages.

## Changes

If a future version needs additional permissions or begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated and the new version's release notes will say so.
